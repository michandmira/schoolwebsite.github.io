Introduction

Founded in 2018 ,NexGen Academy stared with a simple observation: children are surronded by technology but rarely taught how it works. We set out to change that by creating curriculum that is a engaging as a video game as educational as university lab.

Today we serve over 500 student annually across three campures. We are proud to be a leader in STEM education fostering a community of young hacker maker,and dreamer.

Our Misson

To democratize high-level tech education and inspire the next generation of innovators to build a more efficient,inclusive,technologcally advance future for everyone.

Our Vision

To be the global standard for youth technology training, where every student leaves our doors equipped with the tools to solve real-word problems using code and logic.

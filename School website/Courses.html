Our Learning Approach

We utilize a "flipped classroom" model where student engage with interactive marterials before class,allowing the in-person time to be dedicated entirely to building and troubleshooting projects. This maximizes the time spent with instructors. Furthermore, we emphasize "soft skills" like version control(Git),documentation and, team collaboration. We believe that being a great developer is about more than just code --it's about being a great teammate.
